#	**Five Nights At Freddy's**

The Five Nights at Freddy's movie is a 2023 ***supernatural horror film directed by Emma Tammi***. It's based on Scott Cawthon's video game series of the same name and stars Josh Hutcherson, Elizabeth Lail, and Piper Rubio. The film follows a security guard who discovers the animatronics at Freddy Fazbear's Pizza are possessed by the spirits of murdered children. 

**Key Details:**
- **Director:** Emma Tammi
- **Stars:** Josh Hutcherson, Elizabeth Lail, Piper Rubio
- **Release Date:** October 27, 2023
- **Genre:** Supernatural Horror
- **Plot:** A security guard discovers the animatronics at Freddy Fazbear's Pizza are possessed by the souls of murdered children and must survive the night.

#Summary
  A troubled security guard begins working at Freddy Fazbear's Pizza. During his five nights on the job, he realizes that something is wrong with the pizzeria and pretty soon finds the truth about its animatronics.
  
In the year 2000, a desperate young man takes a job at an infamous, long-abandoned pizzeria with a dark past, bringing his little sister along. As they uncover the restless spirits and vengeful animatronics within, they must confront their own haunting memories. With the help of an enigmatic police officer, they battle to save their souls and unravel the sinister secrets lurking at Freddy Fazbear's Pizza.
